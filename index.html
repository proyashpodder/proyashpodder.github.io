    About

    I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Computer Science in the Department of Mathematics, Computer Science and Statistics at Muhlenberg College. My research focuses on IoT security: I study the protocols that Internet of Things devices use to communicate and the platforms that control them, in order to find and prevent vulnerabilities. I am also interested in network and systems security more broadly, including vehicular communication and the formal analysis of security protocols.

    Much of my research is done with Muhlenberg undergraduates. Since Spring 2025 I have mentored nine student researchers. Three of them have co-authored peer-reviewed papers published at reputed IEEE conferences.

    I teach across the computer science curriculum, from introductory programming and data science to data structures, computer networks, and operating systems, and I developed the department’s Introduction to Cybersecurity course.

    Before joining Muhlenberg in 2023, I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Florida International University, where my dissertation focused on automating security for Named Data Networking.

    Whether it is a research collaboration or a friendly chat, feel free to email me or visit me in Trumbower Building, Room 143.
